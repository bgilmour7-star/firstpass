# 0040 · First plans can use small-group skill drills; Phase 3 passing and warm-ups

Date: 2026-11-19 · Decided by: Brett ("we need to find some more passing and warm ups from hockey canada")

## Decision
- **The first plan's skill drill can be "Everyone moves" or "Small groups", never "One at a time".** Passing is done
  in pairs and small groups, so the old everyone-only rule left a Passing pick with nothing to use. Lines stay out:
  they're where U7s wait. The game still has to be "Everyone moves".
- With that, **Hot Potato Partners** (live, Passing) already serves a Passing pick today.
- **Five new U7 drills, In review** (not live until Brett and the head coach approve them in drill review). All are from
  Hockey Canada's U7 plans, re-described in our words with our own diagrams, per the drill sourcing assessment:
  - **Merry-Go-Round** (warm-up, Skating): laps with a skill on the long side. Phase 2 warm-ups and Lessons 18–20.
  - **Puck Explorers** (warm-up, Puck control): stickhandle anywhere, kick the puck up at the lines. Lessons 22–24.
  - **Wagon Wheel** (skill, Passing): a coach in the middle passes to each player, who passes it back. Lessons 17–18.
  - **Puck Triangles** (skill, Passing): pass round triangles, then reverse; backhand as Level 2. Lesson 19.
  - **Pass and Follow Relay** (game, Passing): two-team passing relay. Lesson 19.

## Effect on a new U7 team's first plan (60 minutes, once the drafts are approved)
- Every plan gets a warm-up (Merry-Go-Round), and the leftover scrimmage drops from about 19 to 9–11 minutes.
- A Passing pick gets Wagon Wheel as the skill drill.

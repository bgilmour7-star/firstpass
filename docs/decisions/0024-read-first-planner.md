# 0024 · The planner opens to read; tap a drill to change it

Date: 2026-10-20 · Decided by: Brett (assessment "Planner Simplification", prototype "Planner Test Prototype", treatment A)

## Decision
- **Plans open to read.** Each item is one row: the time, a quiet line saying how it's organised, the drill's name and plain
  name, and a thumbnail. Water, huddles and free skate are one line with their minutes. No minutes steppers, menus or
  "+ Add here" between items until you open one. On the 50-minute sample plan: 72 controls → 28, 6.3 phone screens → 3.4.
- **Tap a drill to open it (treatment A):** the full diagram with **Play** (the rink-side animation; captions only while it
  plays), how it's organised (zones or each group's version, with the existing station and sit-out controls), the levels as
  one row of choices, the cue ("Say: …"), one line of history ("Big hit ×2 · last Oct 17", "New to the kids"), a
  **How to run it ›** row (the drill drawer), minutes and **Swap**, then **+ Add after** with icon buttons: team star, Move
  up, Move down, Remove (with Undo), and ⋯ for Duplicate and Change to. One item open at a time.
- **How a drill is organised, in plain words with a small picture,** everywhere a coach meets it:
  **Everyone rotates** (rotation: every group does all the zones, one after another), **Split by skill** (stations: each
  skill group plays its own version, at the same time), **Whole group** (everyone together).
- **One Add.** Add (plan bar, phone bar, the end of the plan, "+ Add after") opens one picker with every kind of drill, the
  format as a label on each card, and quick Water · 2 min / Small games · 5 min / Huddle · 3 min. The drill decides the kind
  of block. Swap still lists the block's own kind.
- **One message per drill:** a suggestion first, then the first variety warning, then the new-drill limit, then what coaches
  said. A Big hit stays a quiet heart line.

## Why
Brett felt the planner was too complex for novice coaches. The walkthrough found every plan opened as an editing workspace.
Volunteer coaches mostly check a plan and change one or two things, so the default is reading, and editing is one tap away.
